# TTNOverlay Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 03/10/26

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

TTNOverlay is a desktop application that runs locally on your computer. **We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to our servers.**

To function, the application connects directly to third-party services:

* **Authentication (Twitch, Kick, Streamlabs):** When you log in, the application receives an access token from these services. This token is used solely to authenticate your requests (for example, to display viewer counts or moderate chat) and is stored locally on your computer. We do not have access to this token.

* **Chat and Event Data:** The application receives chat and event data (such as subscriptions and donations) directly from the Twitch, Kick, and Streamlabs APIs. This data is processed on your computer to display the overlay and is not sent anywhere else.


* ## 2. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to third parties. Your Twitch or Kick account data is managed directly by those platforms and is governed by their own privacy policies.

## 3. Security

The security of your data is important to us. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS) to communicate with third-party services.

## 4. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
